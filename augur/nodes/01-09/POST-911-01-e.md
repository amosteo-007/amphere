## Resolution Outcome

### Market Channel — **Resolved**

| Asset | Pre-Event (T-1) | T+14 (Sep 25) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| WTI Crude | ~$27.64/bbl | ~$22.40 (−19%) | **Below baseline** — demand destruction overwhelmed initial supply spike |
| S&P 500 | ~$1,092 | ~$1,040 (−10% from pre; −6% from initial reopen rally) | **Not reverted by T+14**; full recovery took ~6 weeks |
| Airlines (UAL/AMR) | $32–35 / $28–32 | −35–50% Sep 17; continued falling | **Never reverted** — UAL bankrupt Dec 2001 |

**Resolution verdict:** Energy price shock resolved below baseline (demand destruction dominant). Equity market shock partially resolved. Airline sector shock **never resolved** — permanently impaired, required bankruptcy/restructuring. This is the key structural market failure from 9/11.

## Overall Status

| Channel | Resolution Status |
|---|---|
| **Market (energy, equities)** | Resolved — reverted or absorbed by T+14 |
| **Market (airlines)** | Unresolved — permanent impairment, bankruptcy |
| **Political/Policy** | Resolved (creation) / Ongoing (operations) |
| **Structural** | Ongoing — permanent institutional change in aviation security |

**Key finding:** The 9/11 demand shock was absorbed by financial markets within weeks (energy, Treasuries, broad equities). The airline sector suffered permanent structural damage. The most durable unresolved outcome is the institutional security architecture — TSA, no-fly lists, cockpit hardening, and associated bureaucratic infrastructure — which persists 20+ years later as a permanent transformation of the aviation operating environment.
