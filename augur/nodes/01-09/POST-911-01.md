# Section (a): Physical / Mechanical Facts
## POST-911-01 | September 11, 2001 Attacks

---

### What Happened

On the morning of September 11, 2001, 19 hijackers affiliated with al-Qaeda seized four commercial passenger aircraft in the eastern United States and used them as weapons, crashing three into major landmarks and a fourth into open ground after passengers resisted.

**The Four Hijacked Flights:**

- **American Airlines Flight 11** (AA11): Departed Boston Logan at ~7:59 AM ET, bound for Los Angeles. Hijacked ~8:14–8:19 AM. Struck the North Tower of the World Trade Center (WTC 1) at **8:46:26 AM ET**. 92 occupants dead. [NIST NCSTAR1; 9/11 Commission Report]

- **United Airlines Flight 175** (UA175): Departed Boston Logan at ~8:00 AM ET, bound for Los Angeles. Hijacked ~8:42–8:46 AM. Struck the South Tower of the World Trade Center (WTC 2) at **9:02:59 AM ET**. 65 occupants dead. [NIST NCSTAR1; 9/11 Commission Report]

- **American Airlines Flight 77** (AA77): Departed Washington Dulles at ~8:10 AM ET, bound for Los Angeles. Hijacked ~8:51–8:54 AM. Struck the Pentagon at **9:37:46 AM ET**. 64 occupants dead; 125 Pentagon personnel dead. Total: 184 dead. [NIST NCSTAR1; ASCE Pentagon Building Performance Report; 9/11 Commission Report]

- **United Airlines Flight 93** (UA93): Departed Newark at ~8:01 AM ET, bound for San Francisco. Hijacked ~9:28–9:31 AM after passengers revolted. Crashed in Shanksville, Somerset County, Pennsylvania at **10:02:17 AM ET**. Intended target: U.S. Capitol (per 9/11 Commission). 44 dead. [NIST NCSTAR1; Flight 93 National Memorial (NPS)]

**Structural Collapse:**

- WTC 1 collapsed at **10:28:31 AM ET** (~1h 42m after impact).
- WTC 2 collapsed at **9:58:59 AM ET** (~56m after impact).
- WTC 7 (a 47-story building not struck by aircraft) collapsed at **5:20:52 PM ET**, due to fire-induced structural failure. [NIST NCSTAR1A]

**Aviation System Shutdown:**

- FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop at ~9:45 AM ET. ~4,500 aircraft in U.S. airspace were directed to land immediately. U.S. commercial airspace remained essentially closed until September 13, 2001 (partial resumption). All major U.S. airports closed through September 12–13. [FAA Chronologies; 9/11 Commission Report]

**Casualties:**

| Location | Deaths |
|---|---|
| World Trade Center (WTC 1, 2, 7, Marriott, ground) | ~2,753 |
| Pentagon | 184 |
| Flight 93 (Shanksville, PA) | 44 |
| All four flights (in-air) | ~266 |
| **Total** | **~2,973–2,977** |

First responder deaths: ~343 (FDNY: 343; NYPD: 23; PAPD: 37; Port Authority: 37). [NIST NCSTAR1; OCME 2005; 9/11 Commission]

### Confidence Score

**0.98**

Justification: The physical facts of September 11, 2001 are among the most thoroughly investigated events in modern history. Four independent U.S. government investigations (9/11 Commission, NIST, ASCE, OCME) corroborate the core facts. NIST provides timestamp precision to the second. NTSB/FDR data independently validate Flight 93 timing. Minor uncertainty (~0.02) attaches to WTC death toll estimates (OCME confirmed 1,582 identifications; NIST modeling suggests 1,350–1,600 per tower), and to precise hijacking onset windows (the 9/11 Commission and NIST differ slightly on when specific flights were seized).

---

### Contested / Unresolved Elements

- **WTC death toll precision:** OCME (2005) confirmed 1,582 identified remains. NIST modeling suggests 1,350–1,600 per tower. The widely cited figure of 2,753 for the WTC complex includes estimated ground-level deaths, elevator occupants, and stairwell victims not recoverable. The true number may never be known precisely.
- **WTC 7 collapse mechanism:** NIST's final finding (fire-induced structural failure) is officially adopted but has been debated in some technical circles. The NIST NCSTAR1A report is the authoritative source; debate is marginal and does not affect the core physical facts of the day.
- **Hijacking onset times:** The 9/11 Commission and NIST NCSTAR1 give slightly different hijacking onset windows (e.g., Flight 175: ~8:42–8:46 vs. confirmed radar track changes). Both sources agree on strike times.

## Pre-Event Market Context

In the two weeks preceding September 11, U.S. financial markets were in a broadly risk-on environment. Equity indices were grinding higher (S&P 500 at ~$1,092 by Sep 10, up from ~$1,085 at the start of the window). Crude oil was in a modest uptrend, rising from ~$26.65 to ~$27.64/bbl — directionally bullish but not stressed. The 10Y Treasury yield was firm at ~4.85–4.90%, credit spreads were compressed, and the dollar was relatively stable. There were no major concurrent macro shocks in the window — the market was not already in a crisis state.

**This is analytically important:** The 9/11 shock arrived during a calm market. The resulting signals are cleaner and more attributable than they would have been during an overlapping crisis.

## Attribution Assessment

**Energy (WTI, Brent):** The oil move is *bifurcated* and must be scored as two separate dynamics:
1. The **initial spike to ~$28.84 on Sep 17** (+4.3%) is directly attributable to the attack — physical disruption of aviation fuel logistics, refinery uncertainty, and short-term supply concerns created an immediate bullish shock. Attribution: **0.80**.
2. The **subsequent collapse to ~$22.40 by Sep 25 (−19% from pre-event)** is a *demand destruction* story — the grounding of all U.S. commercial aviation for days, the cratering of airline stocks, and broader economic uncertainty crushed near-term oil demand. This is still 9/11-related (attribution **0.70**) but contaminated by the broader economic slowdown narrative that was already emerging in Q4 2001.

**S&P 500:** This is a genuinely anomalous signal. The *initial* +6–7% rally on Sep 17 is counterintuitively positive — driven by massive Federal Reserve liquidity injection (50bp emergency cut pre-open) and a reflexive short-covering bounce. This move is partly attributable to the policy response (Fed) rather than the event per se. The subsequent −10% collapse through Sep 25 reflects realized demand destruction and economic fear. Net attribution: **0.65**. The move is real but confounded by the Fed's mechanical response.

**Airline Stocks:** The clearest, most attributable signal in this window. Airlines were the *directly impacted sector* — all four major carriers lost 35–50% on the first reopen day. The signal is *clean* (no confounding: the aviation shutdown was total and unambiguous). Attribution: **0.90**. The only minor uncertainty is how much of the subsequent fall (Sep 17 → Sep 25) was additional event-related repricing vs. market-wide credit concerns spreading to airlines. This is a minor contamination.

**Fixed Income (10Y Yield):** The flight-to-quality bid is clean and immediate — yields fell from ~4.90% to 4.57% in the pre-reopen period (Sep 14). This is attributable to 9/11 (flight-to-safety, uncertainty premium). Attribution: **0.80**. The Fed's 50bp cut on Sep 17 is a confounding factor — some of the yield decline is Fed-driven rather than market-driven — but both the Fed action and the market move were *responses to the same event*, so the confounding is endogenous to the shock itself.

**USD/DXY:** The dollar weakened modestly post-attack (~117.5 → ~114–115). This is *weakly* attributable to 9/11 (confidence shock pulling capital out of U.S. assets) but contaminated by pre-existing dollar trend and late-cycle dynamics in Q3 2001. Attribution: **0.60**.

**Credit Spreads:** Modest widening, especially for Baa and high-yield airline credits. The widening is real and post-9/11 in timing, but Q4 2001 was already a period of earnings recession and credit stress unrelated to the attacks. Attribution: **0.65**.

---

## Antipattern Flags

- **S&P 500 initial direction:** The +6–7% reopen rally is an *inverse* market signal — markets initially interpreted Fed liquidity as more powerful than the shock itself. This is a counter-shock signal, not a direct event transmission. It should be treated as a volitional-market hybrid (Fed response) rather than a pure event signal.
- **Oil's two-phase move:** Cannot be treated as a single attributable event. The spike and the crash have different causal mechanisms and should be scored separately in downstream models.

### Airline Bailout

**Link:** 9/11 attacks → US government airline financial rescue package ($15B)

### Afghanistan Invasion

**Link:** 9/11 attacks → US-led invasion of Afghanistan (Operation Enduring Freedom)

## Notes on Causal Link Methodology

1. **Strength vs. Confidence independence:** As per the ORCA schema, strength and confidence are scored independently. For example, the Afghanistan invasion link has high strength (attacks nearly mechanically required a military response given the political context) and high confidence (the invasion happened, and the causal relationship is well-documented). In contrast, the Al-Qaeda parent link has high strength but moderate confidence, because while the threat environment clearly enabled the attacks, characterizing it as a direct mechanical cause involves some epistemic concession.

2. **Volitional vs. Mechanical distinction:** The key question for each child link is whether the outcome required a specific human decision that could have gone differently. Airline bailout = volitional (decision made); Aviation demand collapse = market-transmitted (aggregate consumer behavior, not a single decision); Afghanistan invasion = volitional (specific administration decision to deploy military force); TSA creation = volitional (specific Congressional action to create agency).

3. **Multi-step chains:** Where causal chains are longer (e.g., 9/11 → economic slowdown → reduced jet fuel demand → airline margin pressure), only the immediate first-order links are scored here. Second and third-order effects are captured in child nodes of downstream events.
