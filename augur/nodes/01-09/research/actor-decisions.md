# Volitional Actor Decisions: Aug 28 – Sep 25, 2001

## FAA / Jane Garvey
- **Sep 11, 2001 (~9:55 AM ET):** Ordered all flights inbound to the U.S. to land immediately; by ~12:15 PM ET, all civilian aircraft in U.S. airspace were ordered to land — the first-ever nationwide grounding of flights.
  - *Counterfactual note (High):* Without Garvey's decision, dozens of aircraft would have remained airborne over a country in active terrorist emergency; mid-air collisions or further attacks via hijacked planes would have been far more likely. High dependency.
- **Sep 14, 2001:** Resumed limited domestic flights under enhanced security protocols.
  - *Counterfactual note (Low):* Resumption timing was contingent on security review but loosely determined by condition of the system; alternatives existed.

## President George W. Bush
- **Sep 11, 2001 (morning/afternoon):** Declared "this is a terrible tragedy" aboard Air Force One; later addressed the nation from the White House at 8:30 PM ET.
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Leadership framing was consequential but communication content had multiple viable alternatives.
- **Sep 14, 2001:** Signed emergency congressional supplemental appropriations (H.R. 2888, $40B initial emergency package).
  - *Counterfactual note (Low):* Broad bipartisan consensus existed; almost certain to pass regardless.
- **Sep 18, 2001:** Authorized the NSA to wiretap without FISA court approval (initial authorization; later retroactively codified).
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Major surveillance policy shift; plausible without 9/11 but timing and scope directly triggered by attack.
- **Sep 20, 2001:** Addressed joint session of Congress; declared "either you are with us, or you are with the terrorists" — framing the war on terror.
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Rhetorical framing shaped global alliances; alternatives existed but this framing dominated.
- **Sep 24–25, 2001:** Signed executive orders: (1) freezing terrorist-linked assets, (2) establishing military tribunals for terror suspects, (3) authorizing CIA covert action in Afghanistan.
  - *Counterfactual note (High):* War cabinet decisions on Afghanistan military intervention directly led to the October 2001 invasion. Without these decisions, al-Qaeda leadership would likely have remained sheltered. High dependency.

## Fed Chair Alan Greenspan
- **Sep 11, 2001:** Ordered the Federal Reserve to provide "adequate liquidity" to banking system; coordinated with ECB and other central banks to limit dollar trading disruptions.
  - *Counterfactual note (High):* Without immediate liquidity injection, interbank markets faced potential seizure; systemic banking crisis averted. High dependency.
- **Sep 17, 2001:** Cut federal funds rate by 50 basis points to 3.0% (emergency inter-meeting action); also lowered discount rate by 50 bps.
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Rate cut signaled confidence and cushioned economic shock; market stabilization probable without it but slower and more volatile.
- **Oct 2, 2001:** Additional 50 bps cut to 2.5% (FOMC meeting).
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Part of coordinated easing cycle; broadly expected given post-attack slowdown.
- **Sep 20, 2001:** Testified before Senate Banking Committee on "condition of financial markets"; described attacks as creating "unusual liquidity demands."
  - *Counterfactual note (Low):* Informational role; policy already enacted.

## Congress
- **Sep 14, 2001:** Passed H.R. 2888 — Emergency Supplemental Appropriations Act for Recovery from and Response to Terrorist Attacks ($40 billion initial emergency package).
  - *Counterfactual note (Low):* Near-unanimous bipartisan vote; virtually certain.
- **Sep 21, 2001:** Senate passed the Air Transportation Safety and System Stabilization Act (part of broader package).
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Direct compensation to airlines and victims; without legislation, airline industry bankruptcy risk was high.
- **Sep 22, 2001:** Congress approved $15 billion airline bailout package — $5B in direct grants + $10B in loan guarantees.
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Without bailout, several major carriers likely would have filed for Chapter 11 within weeks; industry consolidation would have been severe.
- **Nov 19, 2001:** Authorized TSA creation (signed into law as Aviation and Transportation Security Act, H.R. 3150).
  - *Note: Post-Sep 25 scope, but legislative authorization began September–October 2001 debates.*
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Airport security federalization was widely demanded; some form of federal takeover was highly probable regardless.

## Airlines
- **Sep 11–14, 2001:** All major carriers grounded operations entirely (Sep 11–13); estimated 3,000+ flights cancelled daily.
  - *Counterfactual note (High):* FAA grounding was mandatory; airline schedule decisions irrelevant during the shutdown.
- **Sep 14–25, 2001:** Airlines voluntarily reduced schedules by 20–30% as confidence in air travel collapsed.
  - *American Airlines:* Announced 20% capacity cut and 20,000+ layoffs/worst-case workforce reductions by late September.
  - *United Airlines:* Announced 20% capacity reduction and significant workforce reductions.
  - *Delta:* Announced 15–20% capacity cuts.
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Even with partial passenger return, demand collapse was real; cuts would have been deeper/longer without the bailout.
- **Oct 2001:** All major carriers reported mass ticket refund requests; load factors fell below 40% industry-wide.

## Secretary of Defense / DoD
- **Sep 11, 2001 (afternoon):** Defense Department ordered all U.S. military bases to Condition Charlie (Delta equivalent); initiated continuity of government protocols.
- **Sep 14, 2001:** Authorized deployment of Civil Support Teams and activated 56,000 Air National Guard members for air defense.

## Secretary of State Colin Powell
- **Sep 12–20, 2001:** Coordinated NATO Article 5 invocation (NATO's first-ever triggering on Sept 12); built UN Security Council Resolution 1368 consensus.
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* International legitimacy coalition shaped; without this diplomatic groundwork, allied support in Afghanistan would have been weaker.

## New York Mayor Rudy Giuliani
- **Sep 11, 2001:** Declared state of emergency; established field hospitals; coordinated FDNY/PD/NYC response from Ground Zero.
  - *Counterfactual note (High):* Leadership during the crisis prevented secondary panic; Giuliani's decisions shaped the immediate rescue and recovery. High dependency.
- **Sep 12–25, 2001:** Daily briefings; formed Family Assistance Center; managed mass casualty response.
  - *Counterfactual note (Med):* Alternatives existed but Giuliani's pre-9/11 emergency management reputation shaped the institutional response.
