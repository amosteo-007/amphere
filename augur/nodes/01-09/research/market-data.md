## Notes

- **Sep 11–14**: All US equity, derivatives, and most bond markets closed. Only limited OTC/fed funds data available.
- **Data gaps**: Exact closing prices for Brent crude, airline stocks, and DXY on specific dates are incomplete from available web sources. Figures are approximate ranges.
- **Post-close context**: Federal Reserve cut funds rate 50bp on Sep 17 pre-open; emergency liquidity facilities deployed. Markets remained highly volatile through end of September.
