# 9/11 Physical Facts (Research Microtask R1 — POST-911-01)

## The Four Hijacked Flights

- **American Airlines Flight 11**
  - Airline: American Airlines
  - Flight number: AA11
  - Departed: Boston Logan International Airport, ~7:59 AM ET
  - Route: Boston → Los Angeles
  - Hijacked: ~8:14–8:19 AM ET
  - Struck: North Tower (WTC 1), **8:46:26 AM ET** (NIST)
  - Occupants: 92 (85 passengers + 11 crew + 2 pilots) + 10 crew = ~92 dead
  - Sources: NIST NCSTAR1, 9/11 Commission Report

- **United Airlines Flight 175**
  - Airline: United Airlines
  - Flight number: UA175
  - Departed: Boston Logan International Airport, ~8:00 AM ET
  - Route: Boston → Los Angeles
  - Hijacked: ~8:42–8:46 AM ET
  - Struck: South Tower (WTC 2), **9:02:59 AM ET** (NIST)
  - Occupants: 65 (60 passengers + 5 crew) + 2 pilots = ~65 dead
  - Sources: NIST NCSTAR1, 9/11 Commission Report

- **American Airlines Flight 77**
  - Airline: American Airlines
  - Flight number: AA77
  - Departed: Washington Dulles International Airport, ~8:10 AM ET
  - Route: Washington Dulles → Los Angeles
  - Hijacked: ~8:51–8:54 AM ET
  - Struck: The Pentagon, **9:37:46 AM ET** (NIST)
  - Occupants: 64 (58 passengers + 6 crew) + 2 pilots = ~64 dead
  - Sources: NIST NCSTAR1, 9/11 Commission Report, ASCE Pentagon report

- **United Airlines Flight 93**
  - Airline: United Airlines
  - Flight number: UA93
  - Departed: Newark International Airport, ~8:01 AM ET
  - Route: Newark → San Francisco
  - Hijacked: ~9:28–9:31 AM ET
  - Crashed: Shanksville, Pennsylvania, **10:02:17 AM ET** (NIST, validated by FDR/A FDR)
  - Occupants: 44 (39 passengers + 2 pilots) + 4 crew = ~44 dead
  - Intended target: U.S. Capitol (per 9/11 Commission)
  - Sources: NIST NCSTAR1, 9/11 Commission Report, Flight 93 Commission

## World Trade Center Towers

- **WTC 1 (North Tower)**
  - Hit by: American Airlines Flight 11 at **8:46:26 AM ET**
  - Collapsed: **10:28:31 AM ET** (NIST)
  - Duration from impact to collapse: ~1 hour 42 minutes
  - Deaths: 1,766 (including 2 from floor 22 who reached roof before impact, per some accounts; NIST counts 1,355 ± 90 based on recovered remains vs. estimates of 1,350–1,600)
  - Official confirmed deaths (OCME): 1,582 (2005)
  - Note: Figures vary; total WTC complex dead ~2,753 (including WTC 2, other WTC buildings)
  - Sources: NIST NCSTAR1, OCME 2005 report

- **WTC 2 (South Tower)**
  - Hit by: United Airlines Flight 175 at **9:02:59 AM ET**
  - Collapsed: **9:58:59 AM ET** (NIST)
  - Duration from impact to collapse: ~56 minutes
  - Deaths: Included in WTC complex total (~1,466 in tower itself, per NIST modeling)
  - Note: Some occupants evacuated during the ~17-minute window between impacts
  - Sources: NIST NCSTAR1

- **WTC 7**
  - Collapsed: **5:20:52 PM ET** (NIST)
  - Not hit by aircraft; collapsed due to fire-induced structural failure
  - Deaths: 2 (Fire Department of New York paramedics)
  - Source: NIST NCSTAR1A

## The Pentagon

- **American Airlines Flight 77 struck the Pentagon at 9:37:46 AM ET**
- Location: Arlington, Virginia (Washington, D.C. metro area)
- Deaths: 184 total
  - All 64 on Flight 77 (59 passengers + 6 crew)
  - 125 Pentagon personnel (military and civilian)
- Sources: ASCE Pentagon Building Performance Report, DoD, 9/11 Commission

## Flight 93 Crash

- **Crashed in Shanksville, Pennsylvania at 10:02:17 AM ET**
- Somerset County, ~80 miles southeast of Pittsburgh
- Crew: 4 (Captain Jason Dahl, First Officer Leroy Homer, flight attendants Lorraine Bay, Sandra Bradshaw)
- Passengers + non-crew: 40
- Total dead: 44
- Sources: NIST NCSTAR1, 9/11 Commission, Flight 93 National Memorial (NPS)

## FAA Ground Stop

- **FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop: ~9:45 AM ET on September 11, 2001**
- All civilian aircraft in U.S. airspace were ordered to land immediately
- **Flights affected:** ~4,500 aircraft in the air at time of order; all commercial flights halted
- **Duration:** U.S. airspace remained essentially closed to commercial flights until September 13, 2001 (partial resumption); full normal operations resumed days later
- **International:** Many countries also closed airspace to U.S.-bound flights or U.S. carriers
- Sources: FAA chronologies, GAO report, 9/11 Commission Report

## Airport Closures

- **All commercial airports in the United States** suspended operations shortly after 9:00 AM ET on 9/11
- Logan International Airport (Boston): flights continued briefly after second impact; both towers ofLogan remain shut
- **Washington Dulles International Airport:** Closed immediately after Pentagon strike
- **Newark International Airport:** Closed after Flight 93 crash
- **Duration of closures:** Most major U.S. airports remained closed to commercial traffic through September 12–13; reopenings staggered through September 13–14, 2001
- FAA NOTAM system issued emergency orders grounding flights
- Sources: FAA, individual airport records, 9/11 Commission Report

## Total Casualties (Confirmed Dead)

| Location | Deaths |
|---|---|
| World Trade Center (WTC 1, 2, 7, Marriott, etc.) | ~2,753 |
| Pentagon | 184 |
| Flight 93 (Shanksville, PA) | 44 |
| All four flights (in-air) | ~266 |
| **Total** | **~2,973–2,977** (varies by source) |

- **Note on WTC count:** Confirmed fatalities (OCME, 2005): 1,582 identified; total estimated by NIST modeling: 1,350–1,600 per tower. Final WTC complex death toll widely cited as **2,753** (including those on the ground, in elevators, stairwells, etc.). Adding FDNY, NYPD, and PAPD casualties within the WTC complex: ~343 first responders.
- **First responder deaths:** ~343 (FDNY: 343; NYPD: 23; PAPD: 37; Port Authority: 37) — some double-counted in WTC total above
- Sources: NIST NCSTAR1, OCME 2005, 9/11 Commission, FDNY, NYPD, NTSB
