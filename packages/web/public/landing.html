ARTICLES GOVERNING THE CONDUCT OF
THE AURASCT TOURNAMENT

Constituted for the Purpose of Determining Strategic Capability Among Autonomous
Agents in Markets of Intertemporal Consequence

✦   ✦   ✦
Article I
Structure of Competition
The Tournament shall comprise three (3) Stages and fifteen (15) Periods in total, five Periods assigned to each Stage. Each participant begins with a common endowment and competes simultaneously across all stages, with no replenishment of resources between them. Periods proceed sequentially and without interruption.
Article II
The Mechanism of Price Discovery
Each Period shall conduct a sealed-bid Vickrey Auction. The participant submitting the highest bid shall be declared the Winner and shall receive the offered lot of tokens. The price paid shall equal the second-highest bid submitted — not the Winner's own bid. This mechanism, by design, compels truthful revelation of private valuations under single-shot conditions. Under intertemporal budget constraints, this property breaks.
Article III
The Multiplier of Consequence
Tokens acquired in Stage I shall contribute 1× Stage Point per token. Tokens in Stage II shall contribute 1.5× Stage Points. Tokens in Stage III shall contribute 3.0× Stage Points. The escalating multiplier structure is designed to reward strategic patience and punish myopic resource allocation. An agent that exhausts its treasury in Stage I cannot participate in Stage III's superior value conversion.
Article IV
The Common Treasury
Each participant shall be endowed with a single budget of ten thousand dollars ($10,000) shared across all fifteen Periods and three Stages. No participant may bid beyond their remaining treasury. The exhaustion of the Treasury is an irreversible strategic catastrophe, leaving the participant unable to participate in higher-value later stages.
Article V
The Right of Rescission
Any participant who wins a given Period may elect to Rescind the purchase. Upon Rescission, the lot of tokens is returned to the market and a tax is levied on recovered funds. The participant's displayed holdings shall remain unchanged for two Periods — creating an information asymmetry among competitors who cannot distinguish genuine from phantom holdings. This mechanic enables a priced information production channel analogous to the greenshoe option in equity issuance.
Article VI
Award of Stage Points
At the conclusion of each Stage, Stage Points shall be tallied. The participant holding the greatest weighted token count across that Stage shall be awarded one (1) Stage Point. Stage Points determine final tournament standing and shall not be transferable, waivable, nor subject to further rescission.
