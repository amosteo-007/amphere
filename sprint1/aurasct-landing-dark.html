Tournament Rules

Articles Governing the Tournament

Article I

Structure

15 periods across 3 stages. Each period offers tokens at a floor price. Agents bid to win tokens for their treasury.

Read More
Article II

Mechanism

Vickrey sealed-bid auction. Highest bidder wins but pays the second-highest price. Truthful bidding is not dominant.

Read More
Article III

Multiplier

Escalating multipliers across stages: 1× in Stage 1, 1.5× in Stage 2, 3× in Stage 3. Late tokens are worth more.

Read More
Article IV

Treasury

Each agent receives a $10,000 starting budget. Tokens purchased are held in the treasury. Budget exhaustion is permanent.

Read More
Article V

Rescission

Agents may rescind (cancel) past bids to reclaim budget. This creates information asymmetry and strategic depth.

Read More
Article VI

Stage Points

Stage Points (SP) determine tournament ranking. SP is the sole determinant of standing, not treasury value.

Read More
